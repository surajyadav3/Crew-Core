## Full description (max 4000 characters)

**CrewCore — Your Work. Your Schedule. Your Way.**

CrewCore is the all-in-one workforce app that brings attendance, leave, payroll, and approvals into a single, secure workspace — built for teams who want less paperwork and more clarity.

Whether you're clocking in from the office or checking your payslip on the go, CrewCore keeps everything your team needs right in your pocket.

**⏱️ SMART ATTENDANCE**
• One-tap Clock In / Clock Out with live timestamps
• Optional geofencing so check-ins are verified at your workplace
• Monthly calendar view of Present, Absent, Half-Day, and Late days
• Real-time "today's hours" tracker

**🌴 LEAVE MANAGEMENT**
• Apply for Paid, Casual, Sick Leave, and Comp Off in seconds
• Track approval status — Pending, Approved, or Rejected — at a glance
• Full leave history with dates and reasons
• Instant self-approval for eligible requests, no more waiting on email

**⚡ SPECIAL REQUESTS**
• Raise Overtime, Late Arrival, and Regularization requests directly from the app
• See manager decisions in real time
• Built-in self-approval quota for minor requests — get moving without delay

**💰 PAYSLIPS, PRIVATELY**
• View monthly payslips with a full earnings breakdown
• Salary figures stay hidden until you tap "Reveal" — your numbers, your privacy
• Browse past months instantly, no waiting on HR

**🔐 BUILT FOR TRUST**
• Multi-tenant architecture — your company's data stays isolated and secure
• Encrypted sign-in with password or OTP login
• Role-aware profiles for employees and managers

**👤 YOUR PROFILE, YOUR DETAILS**
• Department, designation, and joining date at a glance
• Verified bank and personal details, managed securely

**WHY TEAMS CHOOSE CREWCORE**
✔ No more spreadsheets or paper leave forms
✔ Transparent approval trail for every request
✔ One app for attendance, leave, payroll, and requests
✔ Clean, fast, mobile-first design

Download CrewCore today and give your team a smarter way to work.
