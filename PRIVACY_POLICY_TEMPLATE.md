# CrewCore Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 4, 2026**

## Information We Collect
- **Location data**: We collect your device location only when you clock in/out for attendance purposes. Location is not tracked in the background.
- **Account information**: Email address and name provided during login.
- **Usage data**: Attendance records, leave requests, and payslip views stored in your organization's database.

## How We Use Your Information
- To verify your location for office attendance check-in/check-out.
- To display your attendance history, leaves, and payslips.
- We do NOT sell your data to third parties.

## Data Storage
Your data is stored securely in Supabase (PostgreSQL) managed by your organization.

## Data Deletion
Contact your organization admin to request account and data deletion.
