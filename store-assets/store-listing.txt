--- SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 chars) ---

--- FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars) ---
CrewCore is the employee self-service app that puts your work life in your pocket. Designed for teams that want a simple, fast way to manage daily HR tasks without the paperwork.

━━ CLOCK IN & OUT ━━
Mark your attendance with a single tap. CrewCore uses GPS verification to confirm you're at the right location before logging your check-in — no buddy punching, no disputes.

━━ ATTENDANCE HISTORY ━━
View your full attendance calendar at a glance. See which days you were present, late, or absent with colour-coded daily markers and a timeline of your exact check-in/check-out times.

━━ LEAVE MANAGEMENT ━━
Request leave directly from your phone in seconds. Choose the leave type, set the date range, add a reason, and submit — your manager gets notified instantly. Track pending, approved, and rejected requests all in one list.

━━ PAYSLIP VIEWER ━━
Access your monthly payslips anytime, anywhere. Earnings, deductions, and net pay are clearly laid out. Sensitive figures are hidden by default and revealed with a single tap for your privacy.

━━ SECURE & PRIVATE ━━
Your data is stored securely. Authentication is handled with industry-standard encrypted sessions. You only see your own records — no access to other employees' data.

━━ BUILT FOR TEAMS ━━
CrewCore works alongside your company's HR system. Everything is synced in real time, so your manager always has up-to-date information.

  SCREENSHOTS — WHICH SCREENS TO CAPTURE
======================================================

Capture these 5 screens in portrait mode (recommended: 1080x1920px or your device resolution):

  1. LOGIN SCREEN          — app open before signing in
  2. DASHBOARD (HOME)      — after login, showing stats cards
  3. ATTENDANCE SCREEN     — clock-in button + calendar view
  4. LEAVES SCREEN         — leave list + request button
  5. PAYSLIPS SCREEN       — payslip list (salary hidden)

HOW TO TAKE SCREENSHOTS:
  Option A — Physical Android device:
    • Run the app, navigate to each screen
    • Press Power + Volume Down to screenshot
    • Screenshots saved to your Photos/Screenshots folder

  Option B — Android Emulator (Android Studio):
    • Start emulator: run  npx expo start --android
    • In Android Studio emulator toolbar, click the camera icon
    • Or use:  adb exec-out screencap -p > screenshot.png

  Option C — ADB (device connected via USB):
    adb exec-out screencap -p > "D:\CrewCore\store-assets\screen1_login.png"
    (navigate to each screen and repeat for each)

AFTER CAPTURING:
    Upload all 5 screenshots in Play Console →
    Main store listing → Screenshots → Phone
